# Sample-data safety policy

This repository may contain only synthetic, public, anonymized, or otherwise demonstrably safe
sample data. It must never contain real personal data, customer or citizen documents, employee
records, credentials, tokens, private keys, production exports, or material that could support a deceptive
claim about a real organization or outcome.

Synthetic evaluation and demo material belongs in the following domain boundaries:

- `public-sector/` for safe public-administration scenarios
- `banking/` for safe financial-compliance scenarios
- `energy/` for safe operations and safety scenarios
- `internal-policy/` for safe internal-procedure scenarios
- `evaluation/` for deterministic evaluation fixtures

Before any sample is added, review it for privacy, provenance, and whether its labels and claimed
outcomes are truthful.
